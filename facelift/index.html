What a facelift can do for you

A well planned facelift works on the structures that have moved, so the changes look settled and natural rather than stretched.

  • A defined jawline again

    Jowls are lifted back into place, restoring the clean line from chin to ear.

  • A smoother, firmer neck

    Loose skin and neck bands are tightened, often with a platysma muscle repair.

  • Lifted cheeks, softer folds

    Repositioning the midface eases the heaviness around the nose and mouth.

  • Your own expression

    The deep layers carry the lift, so your face moves and smiles as it always has.

  • Results measured in years

    You will keep looking fresher than you would have without surgery as time goes on.

  • Scars that disappear into folds

Is a facelift right for you?

Age matters less than what you see in the mirror. You may be a good candidate if any of these sound familiar.

Concerns a facelift treats

  • Jowls blurring the line of your jaw
  • Loose skin or visible bands on the neck
  • Cheeks that have dropped, deepening the folds beside the nose and mouth
  • No clear angle between jaw and neck
  • A tired, heavy look that rest and fillers no longer fix

You are likely to be suitable if you

  • Are in good general health
  • Do not smoke, or are ready to stop before and after surgery
  • Want a natural improvement rather than a new face
  • Have tried non-surgical treatments and want a longer lasting change

How a facelift works

Ageing happens in layers. As the deeper supporting tissues loosen, the skin above them follows. Lifting the skin alone does not last and tends to look pulled, so Dr Davide works from the inside out.

Fine, hidden incisions

Incisions follow the natural creases in front of and behind the ear, extending into the temple hairline only when the upper face needs it. They heal as fine lines that are very hard to spot, even up close.

Repositioning the SMAS

The superficial musculo aponeurotic system, or SMAS, is the supportive layer beneath the skin and fat. Dr Davide lifts and secures it by plication or advancement, or uses a deep plane approach where it suits your anatomy. Because the deep layer carries the tension, the skin lies naturally on top.

Redefining the jawline

The direction and strength of each lift are planned around your proportions and expression lines. Restoring the tissue to its original position removes jowls and redraws the jaw, while fat transfer can add back volume where the face has hollowed.

Platysmaplasty for the neck

The platysma is the thin muscle that forms visible bands as it loosens. Reshaping it, sometimes with liposuction under the chin, gives the neck a smooth contour and a crisp angle beneath the jaw. Excess skin is then trimmed and closed with fine sutures.

Your facelift journey

From the first conversation to the day you stop thinking about your recovery, you will know what comes next.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    Dr Davide assesses your skin quality, fat distribution, the SMAS, the neck muscle and the bone structure beneath, and listens to what bothers you most.

  2. 2

    Your personal plan

    You receive a written plan covering the technique, any combined procedures such as fat transfer or neck liposuction, recovery and costs.

  3. 3

    Preparation

    Pre-operative tests, advice on medicines and supplements, and a smoke free period of several weeks to protect healing.

  4. 4

    Surgery day

    Dr Davide marks the lift vectors, then operates under general anaesthetic for two to four hours. Most patients go home the same day or after one night.

  5. 5

    The first two weeks

    Swelling and bruising peak early and settle through the first week. Stitches come out, and by day 10 to 14 most people are back to normal routines.

  6. 6

    Back in the world

    You are ready for social plans within two to three weeks, while the result keeps refining. Follow up appointments continue for a year or more.

What is the best age for a facelift?

There is no ideal age. What matters is the degree of laxity in your face and neck, your skin quality and your general health. Some patients come in their late forties, others in their seventies. Dr Davide will tell you honestly whether surgery is the right step now.

Is a facelift painful?

You are asleep under general anaesthetic during surgery. Afterwards most people describe tightness and numbness rather than pain, which is managed well with simple painkillers for the first few days.

When will I see the final result?

You will see a clear improvement once the early swelling settles after two to three weeks. Residual swelling and numbness fade gradually over the following months, and scars continue to soften for up to a year.

Can I combine a facelift with other procedures?

Yes. Facelifts are often combined with fat transfer to restore volume, liposuction under the chin, or eyelid surgery for a balanced result in one recovery. Your plan will explain what is worthwhile and what is not.

Do I really have to stop smoking?

Yes. Smoking and nicotine reduce blood flow to the skin and significantly raise the risk of healing problems. You will be asked to stop for several weeks before and after surgery.

Will my hairline or ears look different?

Incisions are planned to preserve your natural hairline and the shape of the earlobe, so you can wear your hair up once healed. Careful closure keeps the skin around the ear looking untouched.

Are there non-surgical alternatives?

Fillers, skin treatments and toxins can refresh the face, but they cannot reposition tissue that has descended. If a non-surgical approach would serve you better, Dr Davide will say so at your consultation.
